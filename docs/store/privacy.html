Privacy Policy

Murmur for Windows · Effective 30 October 2026 · Publisher: Morten Brudvik

Murmur is voice typing for Windows: hold a hotkey, talk, and the text is typed into the app you are using. Speech recognition runs entirely on your PC.

What we collect

Murmur collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. Your voice and the text made from it stay on your device.

  • No personal information
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or crash reports
  • No accounts, sign-in, or online services
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected

Microphone

Murmur uses your microphone only while you hold its hotkey, or between the two taps of a hands-free dictation. The recording is held in memory, turned into text on your PC by the Whisper speech model, and then discarded. If you turn on Keep audio recordings in Settings (it is off by default), each recording is also saved as a sound file on your PC, linked to its history entry and deleted with it. Audio is never uploaded.

Keyboard, mouse, and clipboard

To notice its hotkey, Murmur watches keyboard input on your PC, and mouse clicks and scrolling while the hotkey is held. It does not record, store, or send what you type; the only key it saves is the hotkey you choose.

To insert text, Murmur puts it on the clipboard, presses Ctrl+V in the app you are using, and then puts back whatever was on the clipboard before. It does not store or send clipboard contents, and it keeps its own clipboard writes out of Windows clipboard history and cloud clipboard.

Data stored on your device

Everything Murmur keeps is in your user profile’s local app data folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Murmur, and never leaves your device:

WhatContents
SettingsYour hotkey, microphone choice, and the options in Settings
VocabularyThe hint words and replacement rules you add
HistoryEach dictation’s text, its time, and the name of the app it was typed into, kept until you delete it in the app
Audio recordingsOnly if Keep audio recordings is on
Speech modelThe downloaded Whisper model, about 1.6 GB
LogsTechnical events such as start-up, errors, and the timing and length of each dictation and the app it went to; never the words themselves

Murmur’s window is displayed with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows, which keeps its working files in your temporary folder. The window shows only files that come with Murmur.

Uninstalling Murmur does not delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Murmur. Delete that folder to remove your history, settings, recordings, and the speech model.

Network

Murmur connects to the internet for one thing only: on first run, and again only if the model file is missing, it downloads the Whisper speech model from huggingface.co (Hugging Face). That request contains nothing about you: no account, no identifier, no audio, no text. Like any download it reveals your IP address to Hugging Face, which handles it under its own privacy policy. Murmur makes no other network requests.

Microsoft Store

If you get Murmur from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, licensing, and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft may give the publisher aggregated reports about the app, such as install counts and crash statistics from Windows, depending on your Windows diagnostic data settings. Murmur itself adds nothing to them.

Children

Murmur is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
