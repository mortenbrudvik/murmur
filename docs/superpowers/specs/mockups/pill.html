How should Murmur look while you talk?

Pick one: the pill that floats above your taskbar while you dictate. It never takes focus, so your text always lands where the cursor is, and it disappears as soon as the text is pasted.

A tiny capsule with a red dot and a waveform, and no buttons. Let go of the hotkey to finish, press Esc to cancel. It's the quietest option on screen.
